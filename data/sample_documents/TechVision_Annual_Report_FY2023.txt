================================================================================

FY2023 PERFORMANCE COMMENTARY

Revenue growth of 12.5% came primarily from the cloud segment. The on-premises license
business declined slightly as expected as the product portfolio transitions to cloud-first.

Operating margin improved 200 basis points to 22.0%, reflecting:
  - Lower restructuring charges compared to FY2022
  - Improved cloud infrastructure efficiency
  - Headcount optimization in non-strategic functions

Management expects the cloud transition to continue accelerating in FY2024, with
cloud revenue expected to represent more than 50% of total revenue for the first time.

KEY RISKS
  - Macroeconomic uncertainty
  - Competitive pressures from hyperscalers
  - FX headwinds (impacted revenue by approximately $95M)

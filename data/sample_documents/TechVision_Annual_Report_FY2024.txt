PAGE 5 — MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS
================================================================================

REVENUE GROWTH DRIVERS

Management identifies the following primary factors behind the 13.3% revenue growth:

1. Cloud Segment Expansion: The accelerating shift to cloud-delivered products
   drove $485M of incremental cloud revenue. Customers prefer the subscription
   model's predictability and continuous feature updates.

2. Enterprise Customer Growth: We added 2,300 net new enterprise customers,
   reaching a total enterprise customer base of 15,200. Average revenue per
   enterprise customer grew 8% to $280,000.

3. International Expansion: International revenue grew 19% to $1,700M,
   representing 40% of total revenue (up from 38% in FY2023). APAC led
   with 27% growth.

GROSS MARGIN IMPROVEMENT

Gross margin improved 100 basis points from 63.0% to 64.0%, driven by:
  - Product mix optimization: higher proportion of high-margin cloud subscriptions
  - Cost discipline in cloud infrastructure through improved capacity utilization
  - Renegotiated vendor contracts generating $45M in annual savings
  - Automation reducing support costs per customer by 12%

OPERATING LEVERAGE

The 300 basis point improvement in operating margins reflects disciplined
cost management while investing in priority growth areas. R&D increased
to $765M (18% of revenue), focused on AI capabilities and cloud platform enhancements.

RISK FACTORS
  - Macroeconomic uncertainty affecting enterprise IT budgets
  - Competitive pressure from hyperscalers entering our market
  - Talent retention in the AI/ML engineering space
  - Currency headwinds estimated at approximately $85M impact on FY2024 revenue
